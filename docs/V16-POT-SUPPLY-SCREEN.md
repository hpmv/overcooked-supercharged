# V16 native pot supply screen

The completed V16 trace contains substantial native pot downtime, but its largest gaps are not simply player 2 being away. Pot 2 was empty at original stove 17 during gameplay frames 10233–11723 (1,491 frames, 24.85 logical seconds), and pot 7 was empty at stove 19 during 10528–11639 (1,112 frames, 18.53 seconds). Player 2 finished a bun job at 10265 and remained idle in the upper-left pantry until boarding at 10777. Several future prepared hotdogs already occupied center counters within the eight-order planning window. Both pots were already empty before departure, so the existing raw-sausage buffer cannot newly admit in that window: its native admission requires both pots occupied and unsatisfied sausage demand.

Player 2 returned at 11206, immediately boarded for the next ready service head, returned again at 11572, then supplied pot 7 at 11640 and pot 2 at 11724. The corresponding [captured response](../artifacts/v16-supply-snapshots/gf11206.json) has both original pots empty and both central chefs empty-handed. This suggests investigating the existing ready-head/refill decision and planning horizon separately; it does not prove that heating additional speculative food earlier would remain safe or improve the final score.

Across the 16,203 positive gameplay-frame samples, pot 2 was natively empty at its original home for 5,717 frames (95.28 logical seconds); pot 7 for 3,599 frames (59.98 seconds). Of those, 2,597 and 2,026 samples respectively had player 2 outside the controlled upper-left classification or in transit. The same-pot loaded-at-home totals were 138.43 and 160.50 seconds; time spent off the original stove is separate. These are occupancy measurements, not recoverable production time. The exact projection and interval list are in [the hash-pinned native screen](../artifacts/v16-native-pot-gap-screen.json). Its work-lease reconstruction omits no logged normal Work releases, but sampled persistent-lease status can lag; absence of a Work lease is not a complete admission proof.

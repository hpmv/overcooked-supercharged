# Optional cannon firing at completed action boundaries

`--preempt-cannons` enables `CannonBoundaryPreemption`, which defaults to false. The first version considers serial plate-assembly jobs after an ordinary place or condiment-switch action has completed and its neutral frame has actually advanced. It does not interrupt a running action. The central chef must be empty-handed, stationary, under normal control, and have neutral buttons and axes. Sauce, onion and bakery participants, native aiming/dash activity, traffic yields, and near-term cooking/mixing rescue windows exclude admission.

A matching passenger must be observed in native `Load` state with chef control disabled and the cannon not flying. Its linked button and the cannon must be free. The helper needs a full-clearance walking path costing at most one second at its observed native run speed. At least three station actions must remain in the original job, with optimistic remaining travel greater than half a second even at the observed maximum speed. This estimate ignores obstacles and action delays and does not predict net throughput benefit.

The original `Work`, queued action specifications, completion callback and current `OwnedResources` remain intact. A separate firing job leases only the cannon and button and runs ordinary inputs, with dash disabled and a 180-frame timeout. Completion restores the same original work and validates its queue and leases. The normal loop constructs its next action from the observed position; no active action, motion state, game timestamp or native object is reset. Failure aborts the candidate and reports the suspended work.

The heat guard reserves a 3.1-second window including neutral completion margin. It refuses active cooking/mixing that could enter a native warning interval in that window, or leased onion/bakery vessels approaching their nine-second rescue-admission threshold. Parked off-heat/off-mixer vessels do not falsely consume that window.

Captured V7 evidence is in `artifacts/v7-cannon-boundary-review.json` and the post-neutral snapshots at gameplay frames 1250 and 1287. Frame 1250 has no valid button approach and is rejected. At frame 1287, chef 0 has three assembly actions left and a 4.9277-unit clear route to the linked fire button; its observed original suffix took 2.25 seconds. The original firing job started at frame 1345. These observations establish an admissible scheduling experiment, not a demonstrated time saving. A native interruption/resumption trial is still required.

`plannerJobPaused` and `plannerJobResumed` record the original name, player, frame and owned resource IDs. Pause also records the completed boundary, remaining work estimate and button path. Resume records paused duration and observed pose. Normal firing-child start/completion and the eventual original completion remain separately observable.
